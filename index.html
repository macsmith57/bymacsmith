I'm Mac. I'm a music producer, arranger, and audio engineer currently based in Philadelphia, PA.

I recently graduated from Berklee College of Music, where I studied Contemporary Writing & Production. Alongside my studies, I trained as a live audio engineer, working on campus and for venues and production companies in the Boston area.

My portfolio includes personal and professional work, including my jingle for Mingle Mocktails' "Let's Mingle" marketing campaign. I'm comfortable writing across a range of styles — pop, electronic, jazz, big band, and orchestral music.

I'm looking to establish a career in writing music for media and collaborative creative projects. Reach out at kmacsmith.music@gmail.com
